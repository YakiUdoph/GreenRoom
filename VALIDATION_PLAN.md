# GreenRoom Validation Plan

GreenRoom currently has a product thesis and technical foundation, not validated product-market fit. Validation must use real creator evidence rather than invented certainty.

## Creator interview target

Interview 5–10 solo or independent creators before final submission if feasible. Seek variation in audience size, content format, monetization stage, and technical comfort. Do not recruit only AI-tool enthusiasts.

## Research goals

- Identify recurring decisions with the highest mental cost.
- Learn what context creators repeatedly reconstruct.
- Understand current tracking and decision workflows.
- Test whether persistent decision memory is valuable and trustworthy.
- Identify the first external data integration worth building.
- Explore willingness to pay without presenting hypothetical enthusiasm as purchase intent.

## Interview questions

Ask for recent concrete examples before presenting GreenRoom.

1. What creator decisions take the most mental energy each week?
2. How many AI or creator tools do you currently use, and for what?
3. What context do you repeatedly have to explain to AI tools?
4. How do you currently keep track of tools or opportunities worth acting on?
5. Tell me about the last AI recommendation you ignored. What made you ignore it?
6. How do you remember what you tried, rejected, or decided months ago?
7. What information would you want a system to monitor, if any?
8. What would make a decision-support product worth paying for?
9. What would make you distrust it?

After behavioral questions, show the concept and ask:

- Would a system that remembers previous decisions and returns only a few actions worth considering fit any real part of your workflow? Why or why not?
- Where would it need to integrate to be useful?
- Which remembered information should expire or require approval?

## Avoiding leading evidence

- Do not ask only whether the idea sounds useful.
- Prefer past behavior over hypothetical intent.
- Record counter-evidence and rejection reasons.
- Separate creator quotes, observed behavior, interpretation, and product decisions.
- Do not describe five positive interviews as market validation.

## Evidence capture

For each interview record creator segment, current workflow, repeated-context examples, highest-cost decisions, trust concerns, desired signals, willingness-to-pay evidence, and direct quotes with consent. Aggregate patterns only after preserving dissenting cases.

## Moderator / DevRel question

> I'm building GreenRoom as persistent decision intelligence for solo creators: it remembers creator preferences and objectives, works asynchronously, and returns what matters, why it matters, and one practical next action. During Builder testing I found simple bounded Udophia interactions successful while more context-heavy creator-intelligence executions were less consistent. I'm therefore keeping Memory selective and execution bounded. Does this architecture align with how you'd like builders to use persistent Minds, and what would you most want to see proven in the demo?

## Decision gates

- Continue current positioning if creators describe repeated decision-context reconstruction without prompting.
- Reframe the primary job if a different recurring pain dominates.
- Prioritize the integration named in concrete current workflows, not the most impressive API.
- Delay monetization claims until behavior or credible purchase commitment supports them.
- Treat trust, memory correction, and evidence provenance as product requirements, not polish.
