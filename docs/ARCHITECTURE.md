# GreenRoom Architecture

```text
Creator
  ↓
Goal
  ↓
Watch Layer
  ↓
Provider Registry
  ↓
Verified Evidence
  ↓
Relevant Memory
  ↓
Your Mind
  ↓
What Changed / Why It Matters / What To Do Next
  ↓
Feedback
  ↓
Memory
```

Current live provider:

```text
AI-video creator tools → Adobe Blog
```

GreenRoom owns objective binding, evidence retrieval and provenance, relevant-Memory selection, orchestration, run-specific persistence, and feedback. The verified creator Mind and GreenRoom Decision Skill own the personalized reasoning expressed as `WHY IT MATTERS` and `WHAT TO DO NEXT`.

Planned, not implemented: broader creator tools, platform changes, opportunities, monetization, growth/strategy, competition, and audience intelligence.
